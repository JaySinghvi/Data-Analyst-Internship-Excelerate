# Data Analyst Internship - Globalshala Superhero U Campaign Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This repository contains the complete analysis and findings from a 3-week data analyst internship project focused on optimizing Facebook advertising campaigns for Globalshala's Superhero U initiative. The project involved comprehensive performance analysis of 11 marketing campaigns across multiple demographics and geographies to identify underperforming campaigns for potential discontinuation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- **Primary Goal**: Identify the least performing campaign among Globalshala's Superhero U Facebook ad campaigns for potential discontinuation
- **Secondary Goals**: 
  - Optimize resource allocation and reduce marketing costs
  - Enhance overall campaign effectiveness
  - Provide data-driven recommendations for future marketing strategies
  - Maximize return on investment (ROI)

## 📈 Dataset & Methodology

### Campaign Overview
- **Total Campaigns Analyzed**: 11 Facebook ad campaigns
- **Geographic Coverage**: Australia, Canada, UK, Ghana, Nigeria, Pakistan, USA, India, Nepal, Thailand, Taiwan, UAE
- **Target Demographics**: 
  - Students (ages 13-34)
  - Educators and Principals (ages 25-64)

### Key Metrics Analyzed
- **Engagement Metrics**: Clicks, Unique Clicks, Unique Link Clicks (ULC)
- **Reach Metrics**: Impressions, Reach
- **Performance Metrics**: Click-Through Rate (CTR), Unique CTR
- **Financial Metrics**: Cost Per Click (CPC), Cost Per Result (CPR), Total Amount Spent

## 🔍 Analysis Methodology

### Week 1: Initial Performance Analysis
- Compared campaigns based on user interaction metrics
- Analyzed financial efficiency across all campaigns
- Identified preliminary underperforming campaigns (Campaigns 3 & 10)

### Week 2: Deep Dive Analysis  
- Conducted demographic-based analysis across age groups
- Performed geographic expenditure analysis
- Analyzed audience-centric performance (students vs. educators)

### Week 3: Final Comprehensive Review
- Team introduction and project context establishment
- Complete demographic insights exploration
- Final performance spotlight and financial analysis

## 📊 Key Findings

### Campaign Performance Rankings

#### Top Performers:
- **Campaign 2**: Highest reach (46,494), impressions (67,313), and cost-effectiveness
- **Campaign 6**: Lowest CPC and CPR, excellent ROI
- **Campaign 8**: Strong performance across multiple metrics

#### Underperformers:
- **Campaign 3**: Consistently lowest across all metrics
- **Campaign 10**: Second lowest performer
- **Campaign 11**: Minimal reach (2,555) and engagement

### Critical Insights

1. **Geographic Performance**:
   - Multi-country campaigns (Campaign 2) showed superior performance
   - Single-country focused campaigns had mixed results

2. **Demographic Analysis**:
   - 18-24 age group showed highest engagement across campaigns
   - Students demonstrated higher participation than educators/principals
   - Campaign 3 underperformed across ALL age demographics

3. **Financial Efficiency**:
   - Campaign 1: Highest total spend (₹2,333)
   - Campaign 3: Highest CPC and CPR (₹69.24 CPR)
   - Campaign 5: Most cost-efficient (₹838 total spend)

## 💡 Recommendations

### Primary Recommendation: Discontinue Campaign 3
**Rationale**:
- Highest Cost Per Result (₹69.24) among all campaigns
- Lowest engagement rates across all age groups
- Poor ROI compared to resource allocation
- Consistently underperformed in reach, impressions, and click-through rates

### Secondary Recommendations:
1. **Resource Reallocation**: Redirect Campaign 3's budget to high-performing campaigns (2, 6, 8)
2. **Geographic Focus**: Prioritize multi-country campaigns over single-country approaches  
3. **Age Targeting**: Increase focus on 18-24 demographic for maximum engagement
4. **Audience Optimization**: Maintain student-focused campaigns while optimizing educator outreach
